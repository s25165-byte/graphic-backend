ABOUT

We make bicycle frames.
Nothing else.

Chrome started in a garage with a welding torch and an opinion about geometry. Six years later, the garage is bigger. The opinion hasn't changed.

Every frame is 4130 chromoly steel — light enough to race, tough enough to last decades. We build one at a time, because that's the only way to build them right.

PROCESS

How 12 tubes of chromoly steel become the only frame you'll ever need.

Measure

Every build starts with your numbers. Standover, reach, stack — we fit the frame to you, not the other way around.

Weld

4130 chromoly steel, TIG-welded by hand. Every joint inspected twice. No fixtures, no jigs — just experience.

Finish

Powder coat in your color. Headbadge pressed and pinned. Final check, then it ships.
